            What you are looking at

            Every creature carries a genome: its body pixels, colours, size, muscle, armour and diet; its eyes (count, field of view, range) and mouth; its litter size and lifespan; and even its own mutation rate and learning rate. The genome also wires a neural network brain.

            Minds, written in C++

            Brains run in C++ compiled to WebAssembly, all evaluated in one batch call each tick. Each brain is a recurrent network with up to 24 neurons, 51 senses and 13 actions. The recurrent loop gives short-term memory, and 4 gated memory cells hold information for as long as the creature chooses to keep it. Brains also learn from experience through reward-modulated Hebbian learning with eligibility traces: food and safety strengthen the synapses that led to them, and pain weakens them. The Creature tab shows how much each brain has rewired itself during its life. You can make learned wisdom heritable under Mind → Inherit learned wisdom.

            Building, families, conflict

            Creatures pick up stone, sand and wood as they wander, and can decide to place a wall. Walls placed next to their kin's walls are stronger, lone walls crumble, and walls need upkeep. Living among your own walls saves energy, especially on cold nights and in winter. Young born there start stronger, and defenders inside are harder to hurt. Strangers can't pass a species' walls, so they have to break them down. Creatures sense their family (parents, children, siblings), feed hungry relatives first, and sense where home is.

            Fights follow real-world logic. Several attackers on one victim add force roughly with the square of their numbers, so a mob of small creatures can kill a giant. Big bodies out-muscle small ones, and starving creatures fight weakly. Disease breaks out in crowds, spreads by contact, and leaves survivors immune. Forests spread seeds, and saplings grow up into fruiting trees.

            None of this behaviour is scripted. Creatures that find food, shelter and safety live to breed, and their children inherit mutated, recombined genomes. Lineages that drift far enough become new species.

            Transformer cortex

            On top of its learning brain, each creature has a tiny transformer (one layer, two attention heads, written in C++). It remembers the creature's last 12 moments and attends back over them, the way language models attend over earlier words, and sends an 8-number “thought” into the learning brain. The transformer weights are shared by pointer across a whole lineage, like a family's inborn wiring. Only when a mutation hits it does a child get its own copy, so better designs spread through natural selection. Each creature keeps its own private memory of moments, and its learning brain keeps rewiring itself from dopamine.

            Society and money

            Each creature remembers up to 8 others, with feelings from love to hate. Gifts, trades and mating build bonds, and bites build grudges. Brains sense whether the one in front is a friend or a foe, and whether a friend nearby is in trouble, so defending friends, bullying, and life-long partners can all emerge. Food eaten beyond fullness is stashed, shells found on beaches serve as money, and two brain outputs decide when to trade and at what price. Hungry creatures buy food with shells or barter stone for it, and wallets pass to heirs when a creature dies. The Market tab shows prices, a live trade feed, wealth by species, inequality and jobs.

            Nature

            Creatures are diploid, with traits built from many genes, and children inherit through meiosis with crossing-over and copying errors. Weather brings rain, droughts, lightning and wildfire. Temperature falls with altitude and fur adapts to it. Fish live in lakes and amphibians can evolve. Walls stack in 3D, and the richest fruit hangs in the treetops where only climbers can reach it.

            Controls

            Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and press Space to pause. The tools drop food, plant trees, seed new life, or call down a meteor. Everything in Rules changes live.
